## Teaching Focus

My teaching emphasizes:
- Strong **fundamentals of computer science**
- Hands-on **programming and problem-solving skills**
- Exposure to **real-world system concepts** (especially in Operating Systems and Networks)
- Encouraging students to explore **competitive coding and emerging technologies**

These courses are designed to build a solid academic foundation while preparing students for industry and research opportunities.
