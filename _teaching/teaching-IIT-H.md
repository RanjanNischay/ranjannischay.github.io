- Semester: Autumn  
- Years: 2021, 2022, 2023  

### 2. Operating Systems II (CS3523)
- Semester: Spring  
- Years: 2022, 2023, 2024  

### 3. Distributed Computing (CS3520)
- Semester: Autumn 2022, Spring 2024  

### 4. Parallel and Concurrent Programming (CS5300)
- Semester: Autumn 2023  

### 5. Concurrency Control and Transaction Systems (CS5280)
- Semester: Spring 2023  

## Responsibilities

- Conducted **tutorial sessions** and doubt-clearing classes  
- Assisted in **laboratory sessions and system-level experiments**  
- Evaluated **assignments, quizzes, and examinations**  
- Mentored students on concepts related to **Operating Systems, Concurrency, and Distributed Systems**

This experience strengthened my understanding of core computer science subjects and enhanced my ability to explain complex system concepts effectively.
